# Pac AI logo

A frosted-glass tile holding a bold **P** with a lime AI spark in its bowl, plus the
"Pac **AI**" wordmark (Inter Bold + Instrument Serif Italic, the site's typefaces, converted
to outlines so no fonts are needed).

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `pac-ai-mark.svg` | The glass tile on its own: avatars, app icon, social profile |
| `pac-ai-logo-dark.svg` | Tile + wordmark for dark backgrounds |
| `pac-ai-logo-light.svg` | Tile + wordmark for light backgrounds |
| `favicon.svg` | Simplified tile (no blur effects) that stays crisp at 16 px |
| `pac-ai-logo.html` | Live glassmorphism version: real frosted glass over a drifting backdrop |
| `png/` | Ready-made PNGs: mark 512/1024, lockups, favicons 32/48, iPhone icon 180, social share image 1200×630 |

## Notes

- Leave clear space around the logo of at least half the tile's width.
- Below about 32 px, use `favicon.svg` rather than the glass mark.
- The live HTML version supports `?state=hover`, `?t=2` (freeze animations at 2 s) and
  `?reduced=1` (solid fallback). Visitors who turn off transparency or motion get a solid
  tile with no animation.
